Promoted & generated blocks

Hide all YouTube-generated topic shelves is a catch-all: it removes any titled shelf YouTube assembled around a subject (for example "Популярное в вашем регионе"). Shelves without a heading are left alone, since those are your own feed. Shorts and "More topics" keep their own settings, so this one never overrides them.

Background feed preload keeps the home feed flowing without user scrolling. Your subscriptions and the filters hide a lot of videos, so a normal-length feed ends long before your screen is full. This briefly wakes YouTube's own continuation element in the background (~1.2 s per page), so YouTube keeps loading its own feed ahead of you, out of sight. It never moves your scroll position, never reloads the page, and pauses quietly when YouTube has nothing more to give. If your YouTube build has no continuation element, Diagnostics reports it instead of pretending.
